Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before your visit.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking a slot on WhatsApp keeps your waiting time short.

Do you treat children and seniors?

Yes. Our family doctor sees patients of all ages, from newborns to senior citizens.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring old prescriptions, medical reports and a list of medicines you take. A photo ID is helpful.

How much is the consultation fee?

Our fee is shown at the clinic reception and shared on WhatsApp when you book. Follow-up visits within a few days are often discounted.

Can I get a follow-up by phone or WhatsApp?

Yes. For quick questions and report reviews you can message us. For emergencies, please visit the nearest hospital.
